NoCali

Strip the vibration (resonance) calibration from Bambu Studio G-code for the X1C before you send it. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Drop .gcode or .gcode.3mf files here or click to choose
What exactly gets changed?

The X1C start G-code runs the resonance sweep with M970 / M970.3 (shake the axis) and M974 (fit the result). NoCali comments those lines out (; [NoCali] M970 …) so line numbers and everything else stay intact. M975 S1 (turn vibration compensation on) is kept, so the printer keeps using its last stored calibration.

For .gcode.3mf files, every Metadata/plate_N.gcode is patched and its .md5 checksum file is recomputed, otherwise the printer refuses the file.
